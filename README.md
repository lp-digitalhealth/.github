## About This Organization

This GitHub organization is the public technical home of the **Leavitt Partners Digital Health** team. We work at the intersection of federal health policy, interoperability standards, and large-scale digital transformation — partnering with EHR vendors, payers, state health information exchanges (HIEs), and federal agencies including CMS and ONC/ASTP.

Our team convenes and supports public-private alliances that advance the adoption of health data standards across the U.S. healthcare ecosystem. Repositories here host standards artifacts, synthetic test data, implementation guides, proof-of-concept tooling, and alliance work products — all intended to be open, reusable, and standards-grounded.

---

## Focus Areas

- **HL7® FHIR®** implementation and testing
- **Clinical Quality Language (CQL)** and digital quality measures (dQMs)
- **Medical-dental data interoperability** and oral health data exchange
- **Prior authorization interoperability** (Da Vinci CDex, DTR, CRD, PAS)
- **Housing and SDOH payment data models**
- **TEFCA, USCDI, and federal interoperability rule implementation**

## Repositories

### Oral Health & Dental Interoperability

**[ohia-test-data](https://github.com/lp-digitalhealth/ohia-test-data)**
Synthetic test data for loading to FHIR servers for dental interoperability testing. Includes realistic CDT-coded clinical scenarios, patient resources, coverage resources, and Explanation of Benefit (EOB) instances aligned with CARIN Blue Button for Dental.
`License: Apache-2.0`

**[ohia-fhirr6-scratchpad](https://github.com/lp-digitalhealth/ohia-fhirr6-scratchpad)**
Working scratch space for OHIA exploration of HL7 FHIR R6 constructs relevant to oral health data exchange. Supports the HL7 Oral Health Data Exchange Implementation Guide under development (PSS-2772/PSS-274).
`License: CC0-1.0`

**[oral-health-alliance-website](https://github.com/lp-digitalhealth/oral-health-alliance-website)**
Source for the Oral Health Interoperability Alliance public-facing website.
`Language: HTML | License: Apache-2.0`

---

### Clinical Quality Language (CQL)

**[cql-wiki-poc](https://github.com/lp-digitalhealth/cql-wiki-poc)**
Proof-of-concept for a CQL knowledge hub — a collaborative wiki environment for CQL library authoring, cross-engine validation, and shared measure logic. Supports the DQIC community and HL7 Connectathon work.
`License: Apache-2.0`

---

### Housing & SDOH

**[housing-data-coalition](https://github.com/lp-digitalhealth/housing-data-coalition)**
Hosts the **Medicaid Payment Data Model for Housing Support Services**, drafted by the Housing Data Payment Coalition. Provides a reference data model for coding and submitting housing-related Medicaid claims and encounters.
`License: Apache-2.0`

---

### Pharmacy Interoperability

**[carin-rtpbc-testdata](https://github.com/lp-digitalhealth/carin-rtpbc-testdata)**
Test data for the CARIN Real-Time Pharmacy Benefit Check (RTPBC) Connectathons. Supports implementer testing against the CARIN RTPBC Implementation Guide.
`License: CC0-1.0`
